Communitarian IndividualismCommunitarian individualism is the American expression of liberty--not as unbounded autonomy, but as liberty constrained by moral obligation, civic duty, and belonging. It describes a lived synthesis in which liberty is conditioned by moral equality, and equality is energized by personal responsibility. It is freedom lived in community--the cultural and moral precondition for the American Ideology itself.
